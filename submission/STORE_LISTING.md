# Chrome Web Store Listing Draft

## Name

Serendipity

## One-line summary

Add serendipitous pivots to Google Search so your next click is not always more of the same.

## Description

Serendipity adds an intervention layer to Google Search designed to make search
less self-reinforcing.

Instead of only following your usual query habits, Serendipity introduces
deliberate drift:

- Re-run a Google search with reduced personalization
- Surface adjacent, contrarian, reflective, and random pivots
- Auto-inject a remix into exploratory queries if you opt in
- Skip utility and navigational searches like `gmail`, `slack login`, or
  `google slides`
- Let you tune how far the remix should drift from your original intent

Serendipity does not spoof your identity or mask your account. It works at the
query and results-page layer to create room for novelty, disagreement, and
serendipitous discovery.
